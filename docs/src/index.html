@ratmath/chaos

Chaos without rounding error

Dynamical systems are where floating point fails fastest: a chaotic map doubles every rounding error at each step. Here orbits are exact or enclosed, periodic points are algebraic numbers, and statements about stability and bifurcation are proved.

Planned, not built yet. This site is the frame the package will grow into. The full plan is in the library roadmap, and the RatMath Shelf page lists what exists across all the libraries.

What it will cover

  • Maps of the line: exact orbits, periodic points as algebraic numbers, stability.
  • Bifurcation diagrams, Lyapunov exponents and symbolic dynamics.
  • Maps of the plane, and flows with sections, periodic orbits and bifurcations.
  • Iterated function systems, Cantor sets, dimensions, L-systems and cellular automata.
  • Julia and Mandelbrot sets and Newton basins.
